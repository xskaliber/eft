# 11. Using the web portal

**For:** partner staff with the **Web User** persona. Share this page with them.

## Sign in

Open the URL your administrator gave you and sign in with your username and password. After first login, open your **Profile** and change your password (you'll need to enter the current one).

## Dashboard

The **Dashboard** summarizes your **inbound** and **outbound** file activity.

- **Inbound**: files a trading partner sent to you
- **Outbound**: files uploaded for delivery to a partner

## My files

Go to **Files**.

- Use the direction filter to switch between inbound and outbound.
- **Download**: click a file. A temporary download link is generated and **expires after a short time**, so click again if it has expired.
- **Upload**: use the upload control to send a file for delivery.

You only see files for your own organization.
